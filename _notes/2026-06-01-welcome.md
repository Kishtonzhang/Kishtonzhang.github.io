This is a sample note in the `_notes/` collection.

**To add a new note:** create a `.md` file under `_notes/` with the YAML header above, then commit to the `master` branch. It will show up on [Notes](/notes/).

**To share a PDF:** upload to `files/notes/` and link it from this page or from your homepage.
